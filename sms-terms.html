SMS Terms & Conditions.

Program terms for the Therapy by David Appointment Booking SMS program. By texting our practice or providing your number through a referral, you agree to these terms.

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Program Name

TherapyByDavid Appointment Booking.

Program Description

The TherapyByDavid Appointment Booking program sends one-way and limited two-way SMS messages from David Robles, LMSW — a licensed therapist in private practice — to prospective clients who have submitted a referral or inquiry through Open Path Collective, Mental Health Match, or Psychology Today.

Messages introduce the therapist and include a link to a Calendly page where the recipient can book a free 15-minute consultation. We may also reply to questions you send in response.

Message Frequency

Approximately 1–2 messages per referral. Across all recipients combined, the program typically sends 5–15 messages per week.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard messaging rates from your wireless carrier apply to every message sent or received. Check your mobile plan for details.

Opt-Out Instructions

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages.

If you opt out and later want to receive messages again, you can text us first or submit a new referral through one of our directory listings.

Help Instructions

Reply HELP for help, or contact david@therapybydavid.com or call (832) 544-2562.

Supported Carriers

This program is supported by all major U.S. wireless carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon Wireless, Sprint, Boost, U.S. Cellular, MetroPCS, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile information will NOT be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers and SMS content collected through this program are used solely to communicate with you directly. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your number to outside parties for their own messaging.

For full details on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and the account holder or authorized user of the mobile number you provide. By participating, you confirm both.

Not for Emergencies

SMS is not monitored 24/7 and is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health emergency, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911.

Changes to These Terms

We may update these SMS Terms from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this program? Email david@therapybydavid.com or call (832) 544-2562.
