Quick Answers

A few things worth knowing.

Is this a real clinical assessment?

Yes — it uses two validated, peer-reviewed screening tools used routinely by doctors and therapists. They are not a diagnosis, but they are the same instruments used to start clinical conversations about mental health.

Who can see my answers?

Your responses are private. Your name and email are used to display your results — they are not sold, shared, or used for marketing. David does not see your individual question responses.

What happens after I see my results?

Nothing happens automatically. You'll see a clear explanation of your scores, and if it makes sense, an option to book a free 15-minute consultation with David. No pressure, no follow-up campaign, no obligation.
