It's Not Weakness. It's Not A Choice.

Therapy for the depression that quietly takes over.

It doesn't always look like crying in a dark room. Sometimes it's just flat — going through the motions, answering when spoken to, getting less and less out of the things that used to matter. The energy is gone, the sleep is off, and a quiet voice keeps telling you that you're not enough. Depression is a real medical condition, not a character flaw, and it is more than "just having the blues."

I'm David, a Licensed Psychotherapist in Pasadena, Texas. I work with adults whose depression doesn't always show on the outside — people still showing up to work and family while feeling hollow underneath. Therapy here isn't endless venting that circles the same heaviness. It's a focused, practical space to understand what's driving the low mood and build real tools to start moving again.

You don't have to wait until you can't get out of bed to take this seriously. If you want to understand the mechanics first, I wrote a plain-language guide on how depression works. And if the low mood traces back to something older, this work pairs naturally with trauma therapy.

How The Work Moves

Evidence-based methods to lift the weight.

I use structured, well-researched approaches built to actually move depression — not open-ended talk that keeps you stuck in the heaviness. We start with what's fueling the low mood, build tools you can use in real life, and keep it tied to your day-to-day. Three methods anchor the work:

CBT

Interrupt the negative loop

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Depression runs on thinking patterns — "I'm worthless," "nothing will change," "it's hopeless." We find those patterns and build practical ways to challenge and shift them. The gold-standard approach for depression, and it works.

BA

Get moving before you feel like it

Behavioral Activation

Depression tells you to wait until you feel better to do anything — but the doing is what brings the feeling back. We rebuild small, doable activity so momentum and energy start to return.

ACT

Reconnect with what matters

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

When everything feels flat, it's easy to drift from the things that give life meaning. This work helps you make room for the hard feelings and take real steps back toward the life you actually want.

Common Reasons People Reach Out

What we work on together.

You don't need a formal diagnosis or a breaking point to start. These are the themes that bring people in most often.

Low Mood & Numbness

The Heaviness That Won't Lift

Persistent sadness, emptiness, or a flat numbness that colors everything. We work to understand what's underneath it and build a way back toward feeling like yourself.

Loss Of Interest

Nothing Feels Worth It

The things you used to enjoy don't move you anymore. We rebuild connection to activity and meaning so the world stops feeling gray and far away.

Hopelessness & Self-Criticism

The Voice That Says You're Not Enough

"I'm worthless," "nothing will change," "it's my fault." We get underneath the harsh inner voice and shift the pattern that keeps the low mood going. More on depression →

Fatigue & Sleep

Tired No Matter What

Drained energy, sleeping too much or too little, a body that feels like it's moving through mud. We address the physical side of depression so the basics stop feeling impossible.

Withdrawal & Isolation

Pulling Away From Everyone

Not answering texts, canceling plans, staying inside. We gently interrupt the isolation loop that quietly deepens depression and keeps you stuck.

Grief & Life Change

After Loss Or Upheaval

Loss, a breakup, a job, a season of life that ended. We make room for grief and find steady footing again — without rushing or minimizing what you've been through.
