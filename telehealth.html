Common Questions

What people ask before starting.

Is online therapy actually effective?

Yes — research consistently shows telehealth therapy produces outcomes comparable to in-person sessions for anxiety, depression, trauma, and stress. The therapeutic relationship is what matters most, and that translates through a screen.

What do I need to get started?

A phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone. A private space where you can speak freely. A decent internet connection. That's it — no software downloads required.

Is it confidential and secure?

All sessions are conducted over a HIPAA-compliant, end-to-end encrypted platform. Your sessions, notes, and information are fully protected under the same privacy laws as in-person therapy.

Do you take insurance for telehealth?

Yes — most major insurance plans cover telehealth therapy the same way they cover in-person sessions. I accept Aetna, United Healthcare, Cigna, BCBS, and others. Check the Contact page for the full list.

Can I switch from telehealth to in-person?

Absolutely. I have in-person offices in Pasadena and Webster. You can mix and match based on what works for your schedule — some clients do one in-person session a month and the rest online.

Where in Texas can you see clients?

Anywhere in the state of Texas. I'm licensed in Texas, so as long as you're physically located in Texas during our session, we're good to go — regardless of what city you live in.
