Hi, I'm David.

I'm a licensed psychotherapist based in Texas. I work with adults who are quietly carrying a lot, anxiety, burnout, past trauma, relationship stress, or just the weight of trying to hold everything together.

My approach is direct, practical, and collaborative. I'm not the kind of therapist who sits in silence and waits. I'll ask real questions, offer honest feedback, and work alongside you to better understand what's happening and how to move forward.

As a veteran, I know what it means to push through things without talking about them. That experience shapes how I work: no fluff, no judgment, just honest conversation about what's actually going on.

Whether you're completely new to therapy or returning after a difficult experience, my goal is to create a space where you can talk openly, feel understood, and begin making meaningful changes.
