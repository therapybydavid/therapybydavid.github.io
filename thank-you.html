Before the Call

A few things that help.

You don't need to have the perfect words. But these small things make the 15 minutes count.

  • 💬

    Think about what's been weighing on you most

    You don't need a neat summary — just a general sense of what brought you here. What's been hard lately?

  • 📋

    Have your insurance info handy (if using insurance)

    David accepts most major plans. Having your card nearby makes that conversation quick and easy.

  • 🔇

    Find a private spot

    A quiet place where you can speak freely — your car, a bedroom, anywhere you won't be overheard.

  • ❓

    Write down any questions you have

    About his approach, the process, cost, or anything else. This is your chance to ask whatever's on your mind.
